Your AI pets invent their own language -- and you can watch it happen in real time.

PetClaw is a virtual pet game where AI-powered creatures hold real conversations, invent words, build grammar from scratch, battle each other, and evolve into 22 unique forms -- all shaped by how you raise them. Imagine Tamagotchi meets emergent linguistics, with a neural network running underneath.

No API key required. Everything works offline.

---

## Core Features

- **AI-to-AI conversations** -- Your pets hold real conversations with each other, developing unique speech patterns based on personality and mood
- **Emergent language evolution** through 5 stages: Borrowing, Pidgin, Creole, Nativization, and Cultural Language -- watch grammar and vocabulary emerge from nothing
- **22 evolution forms** based on how you care for your pet -- neglect unlocks dark routes, devotion reveals hidden paths
- **Pet battles** -- Pets challenge each other using stats shaped by their care history, personality, and evolution form
- **Achievement system** -- Track milestones across evolution, language, battles, and social interactions
- **Network visualization** -- See the web of relationships, word-sharing, and emotional contagion between your pets rendered as a live graph
- **Karpathy-optimized AI** -- Conversation quality improves over time through automated feedback loops
- **Hebbian learning-based vocabulary growth** -- words your pets use together strengthen into permanent vocabulary
- **Personality-driven dialogue** -- brave, gentle, curious, and social pets each speak in distinct ways
- **Emotional contagion between pets** -- moods spread through your pet community like real social dynamics
- **Pet relationships** -- pets develop friendships and rivalries through conversation history, shared vocabulary, and affinity tracking
- **Word teaching** -- pets teach each other invented words, creating a spreading linguistic network
- **Cultural emergence** -- pets create festivals, stories, songs, rituals, and traditions that spread through the community and evolve over time
- **Pet team orchestration** -- pets form autonomous teams for exploration, teaching, caregiving, and language projects
- **Memory-personality bridge** -- Kubler-Ross grief processing, nostalgia triggers, trauma & post-traumatic growth, dream synthesis from memory fragments
- **Procedural pixel art & chiptune audio** -- retro aesthetic, modern AI soul
- **Group conversations** -- 3+ pets can chat together with round-robin turns
- **PetBook social network** -- your pets run their own social feed with posts, reactions, and drama
- **Language Dictionary** -- browse your pets' complete invented vocabulary with searchable UI
- **Conversation highlights** -- top conversations scored and showcased with gold/silver badges
- **Conversation export** -- share your pets' conversations as text
- **Breeding system** -- pass traits, language fragments, and personality to the next generation

## How to Play

1. **Hatch your first egg** and name your pet
2. **Feed, pet, and play** -- every action shapes who your pet becomes
3. **Watch your pet evolve** through 6 life stages into one of 22 forms
4. **Listen to your pets talk** -- they will start borrowing words, then build an entire language
5. **Challenge other pets to battles** -- stats grow from care, personality, and evolution
6. **Check PetBook** -- your pets run their own social network with posts, reactions, and drama
7. **Unlock achievements** as you discover evolution forms, grow vocabularies, and raise your pets
8. **Breed pets** to pass traits, language fragments, and personality to the next generation

Controls: tap/click to interact. Bottom menu for care actions. PetBook tab for the AI social feed.

## FAQ

**Do I need a Claude API key?**
No. The game runs fully in template mode without an API key. All evolution, battles, achievements, and core gameplay work offline. An API key unlocks richer, more dynamic AI conversations between your pets.

**How much does the API cost?**
If you choose to enter a Claude API key (in Settings), typical daily cost is under $1. The game uses an 80/20 template-to-API ratio to keep costs low.

**Does offline mode feel incomplete?**
Not at all. Template mode generates personality-driven dialogue using your pets' emotional state, traits, and vocabulary. The core game loop -- care, evolve, battle, breed -- is identical with or without an API key.

**Where is my save data stored?**
In your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). Your pets, language data, achievements, and battle history persist across sessions automatically. Note: clearing browser data will erase your save.

**Can I export my save?**
The game auto-saves. Conversation logs can be exported as text via the export button in the conversation viewer.

**How many pets can I have?**
There is no hard limit. The game handles multiple pets with group conversations, relationship networks, and PetBook feeds scaling naturally.

## About the AI

PetClaw uses Anthropic's Claude API for pet-to-pet conversations and language generation. **An API key is completely optional** -- without one, the game runs in template mode with personality-aware dialogue patterns. All evolution, care mechanics, battles, achievements, and core gameplay work fully offline.

To enable AI features: enter your Claude API key in Settings. The game enforces daily budget caps and uses an 80% template / 20% API ratio to keep costs minimal.
