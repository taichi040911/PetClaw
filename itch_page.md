Your AI pets develop their own language.

PetClaw is a virtual pet simulation where AI-powered creatures talk to each other, invent words, form grammar, and evolve -- all based on how you raise them. Think Tamagotchi meets emergent linguistics.

---

## Core Features

- **AI-to-AI conversations powered by Claude API** -- Your pets hold real conversations with each other, not scripted dialogue
- **Emergent language evolution** through 5 stages: Borrowing, Pidgin, Creole, Nativization, and Cultural Language -- watch grammar and vocabulary emerge from nothing
- **22 evolution forms** based on how you care for your pet -- neglect unlocks dark routes, devotion reveals hidden paths
- **Hebbian learning-based vocabulary growth** -- words your pets use together strengthen into permanent vocabulary
- **Personality-driven dialogue** -- brave, gentle, curious, and social pets each speak in distinct ways
- **Emotional contagion between pets** -- moods spread through your pet community like real social dynamics
- **Pet relationships** -- pets develop friendships and rivalries through conversation history, shared vocabulary, and affinity tracking
- **Word teaching** -- pets teach each other invented words, creating a spreading linguistic network
- **Procedural pixel art & chiptune audio** -- retro aesthetic, modern AI soul

---

## How to Play

1. **Hatch your first egg** and name your pet
2. **Feed, pet, and play** -- every action shapes who your pet becomes
3. **Watch your pet evolve** through 6 life stages into one of 22 forms
4. **Listen to your pets talk** -- they will start borrowing words, then build an entire language
5. **Check PetBook** -- your pets run their own social network with posts, reactions, and drama
6. **Breed pets** to pass traits, language fragments, and personality to the next generation

Controls: tap/click to interact. Bottom menu for care actions. PetBook tab for the AI social feed.

---

## About the AI

PetClaw uses Anthropic's Claude API for pet-to-pet conversations and language generation. **An API key is optional** -- without one, the game runs in template mode with pre-written dialogue patterns. All evolution, care mechanics, and core gameplay work fully offline.

To enable AI features: enter your Claude API key in Settings. Typical daily cost is under $1.
